Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who this covers

VexDashboard is a personal tool used only by its owner, Nick (TWITCHYGAMEZ). It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about anyone other than the owner.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in to his own accounts, VexDashboard accesses only his own data through each platform's official API:

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar), account statistics (such as follower count) and the list of his own videos with their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares). If posting is turned on, it uploads his own clips to his own account or drafts.
  • YouTube: his channel's statistics, his own videos and their statistics, and where views came from (read-only).
  • Twitch: his channel's followers, stream statistics and events (follows, subscriptions, raids).
  • Facebook and Bluesky: posts and statistics for his own Page and account, and posting his own content when he chooses.

How it's used

The data is shown only to the owner, in his own dashboard, so he can see how his content is doing and plan what to post. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising or given to anyone else.

Where it's kept

Data is stored on the owner's own computer and his own private server. Sign-in tokens are kept there too and are only used to call the platforms' APIs on his behalf.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account at any time (for TikTok, in TikTok's settings under security and permissions, or by removing the app), and delete the stored data from his computer and server.
