VexDashboard

VexDashboard is a private tool made and used by Nick, the streamer behind TWITCHYGAMEZ, to manage his own gaming content. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up or use it.

What it does

  • Turns his own stream recordings into short clips and helps him review them.
  • Shows his own follower and video statistics from TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, Bluesky and Facebook in one dashboard.
  • Posts his own clips and posts to his own accounts, only when he chooses to.
